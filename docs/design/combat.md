# Game design: Combat

Part of the [design.md index](../design.md). Record decisions here, in the topic they belong to.

## Combat
- **Hitscan** (bullets travel instantly, as a raycast). No bullet travel time or drop.
- **Collision** is simple: boxes (rotated about the vertical axis only), the terrain height grid, and ramps for stairs. No physics engine.
- **Weapon attachments** use fixed slots, like Battlefield 4 or Black Ops 2: **optic, muzzle, magazine**, with underbarrel or laser possible later. Each gun defines its slots and where they sit on the model; each attachment says which slot type it fits.
  - **Fit (decided 2026-10-26, session 6b):** an attachment fits every gun with its slot, unless it lists the guns it fits. Optics and muzzles usually fit anything; magazines list their gun (the 45-round 7.62×39 mag fits only the AKM).
  - **Stat changes** multiply the gun's spread (hip and aimed), recoil, aim time, reload time and loudness; a magazine adds rounds. All the attachments' changes stack. Since 2026-11-04 recoil can also be changed up and sideways separately (`recoilUp`, `recoilSide`, on top of `recoil`), and **ergonomics** multiplies how fast recoil wears off (see Recoil below).
  - **Changing the magazine** puts rounds that no longer fit back with your spare ammo, and stops a reload in progress.
- **Magazines:** one magazine attachment per gun (for example, "extended mag" raises capacity). Reloading takes from the ammo you carry. No individual mags, no loading rounds.
- **Suppressors** reduce a shot's hearing radius (see AI). Session 6b: every gun has a `loudness` (how far away a shot is heard, in meters; rifle 180, pistol 120), and each shot carries it for AI hearing. The suppressor multiplies it by 0.3 and hides the muzzle flash. A muzzle brake is the opposite trade: 30% less kick up and 20% less sideways, 20% louder. The suppressor's weight costs 10% ergonomics (2026-11-04). A **compensator** (the user's call, 2026-11-04) only cuts kick sideways, by 20%, with no loudness cost: cheaper (2,500, uncommon), in the attachments and military loot and sold by Kovacs.
- **More guns (2026-10-30):** four more, each with one clear job. Numbers are first guesses, to tune in play.
  - **Pump shotgun** (the **MP-133** since 2026-11-05, see below; 12-gauge buckshot; in zombies mode dragon's breath, see Zombies): since 2026-11-05 each shell fires **8 pellets of 33** in a 3° cone (Tarkov's 7 mm buckshot, 8 × 39), so all of them landing is 264 and even half kills an unarmored chest; range thins it out fast. It was 9 × 14 (126). Pump action is `semi` at 70 rpm, 6 shells, reloaded all at once for now (one shell at a time would suit it better later). Optic slot only.
  - **Sniper rifle** (**deleted 2026-11-05**, the user's call, as the last generic gun: the SV-98 took its place in zombies mode's upstairs wall gun and mystery box, and profile format 7 turns stored ones into SV-98s on their own round; it was 7.62 mm): bolt action, `semi` at 40 rpm, 5 rounds, 95 damage (one shot to an unarmored chest), 400 m range, very accurate aimed and poor from the hip, slow to aim and draw. The loudest gun (260 m). Takes the 4× scope and muzzle devices. Found in raids only; its rounds are sold.
  - **Submachine gun** (9 mm; since 2026-11-05 the **K10**, a Vector look-alike, see below): 1,200 rpm with light kick, 25 rounds; low damage per round, a lot per second up close. Optic and muzzle slots.
  - **Crossbow** (bolts): heard only 8 m away (quieter than walking), one bolt then a 2.8 s reload, 80 damage and a high bleed chance, but weak against armor. Hitscan like everything else, so no bolt drop; its short 80 m range stands in for that.
  - Scavs can now carry the shotgun or the submachine gun; all four are in the military loot table; Kovacs sells the shotgun, the crossbow and every new kind of round.
- **Laser:** a short see-through red line plus stat changes. No collision, and the AI doesn't notice it.
- **Firing (session 6):** weapons are `auto` (fire while held) or `semi` (once per pull), with a rate in rounds per minute. Shots stray randomly within a cone: wide from the hip, tight when aimed; aiming in takes the gun's aim time. Each shot kicks the view up (and a random bit sideways), and the view settles back by itself; the kick is the simulation's, so co-op sees the same. **Firing or aiming stops a sprint**, and firing ends sprint lock as aiming does. You can't fire on a ladder or while reloading. Pulling the trigger on an empty gun starts a reload. All weapon stats are in the item files (`content/items`).
- **Aiming:** magnified scopes use full-screen zoom (narrower field of view, a scope mask and a reticle), like other mobile shooters. Red dots (Coyote-style) have no zoom, just a reticle. Reticles and zoom levels are set in the item editor. Each gun model defines a sight line so the reticle lines up with the camera when aiming.
  - Session 6b: a scope's view **snaps to its zoom behind the mask at 90% aimed** (before that it zooms like iron sights), and the gun is hidden. Looking turns the view more slowly by the same factor, so it moves as fast on the screen. A red dot's reticle shows at 95% aimed, when its frame has come up to the eye. **No crosshair at all (the user's call, 2026-11-05, as in Tarkov):** there's no dot from the hip either, and no hit-marker flash or sound: nothing says a shot landed or killed but the target itself (the hit and kill sounds went the same day). Hip fire, the knife, taking items and auto-fire still aim along the screen's center, just unmarked.
  - **Sights move, and shots follow them (the user's call, 2026-11-04):** aimed, a player's gun sways (about 0.35° side to side and 0.25° up and down standing still, twice that walking, ×3 with a broken arm and ×5 with both) and lags behind turning (at most 0.4° and 0.25°, settling faster on guns with a short aim time; cut from 1.5° and 1° on 2026-11-11 for mouse and thumb alike, as the lag missed two in three rounds tracking a strafing target at 10 m). Recoil climbs the sights only a few degrees before the view takes over (see Recoil → View vs. gun). The reticle, the scope mask and iron sights all sit where the gun points, and bullets go there. The hip crosshair stays centered with no sway and full recoil on the view. Enemies have no sway. Tune on a real iPhone: thumb aiming is the risk.
  - **Iron sights (2026-11-09, the user's request after "the back block completely covers the front iron sight"):** aimed, the eye sits on a gun model's `sight` height, 0.3 m behind the gun's origin (0.34 m on a pistol), looking straight along −z, so the irons are built round that line: the **front post's tip is on it**, and the rear is a **notch whose ear tops are on it** or a **square aperture centered on it**. Sizes are set by the angle they cover from the eye, the same on every gun: post 0.7° wide, notch 2.1° wide and 1.7° deep with 1.2° ears, aperture hole 3.6° across in a 0.7° frame, the post tall enough to fill the notch or hole; front wings sit 1° below the tip so the post is the highest thing in the picture, and a hood (HK guns, bolt guns) clears it by 1.1°. Nothing nearer the eye may reach the line inside the notch or hole. Pistols have three white dots; the MP-133 a brass bead. Rear sights fold away under an optic; guns without real irons (AUG, AWM) get flip-ups that do too. The parts are named (`rear sight`, `rear sight ear`, `rear sight ring`, `front sight post`, `front bead`, …) in the group `iron sights`, and a content test holds them to `sight`.
  - **Recoil has to outrun recovery (the user's call, 2026-11-04):** recovery runs during a burst, so a gun only climbs if `up` is more than `recovery × 60 / rpm`; before this every automatic recovered more than it kicked and fired on one spot. New numbers (up / side / recovery): AKM 1.8 / 0.7 / 11, SMG 0.7 / 1 / 9, pistol 2.4 / 0.9 / 10, shotgun 6 / 1.8 / 9, sniper 5 / 1 / 8, crossbow 0.9 / 0.3 / 8, grenade launcher 8 / 2 / 8, flamethrower 0.3 / 0.4 / 2.5. **SMGs kick more sideways than up; assault rifles more up than sideways.** Admin spawner guns are unchanged. Enemies use the same numbers but cancel all of it (see AI). *Recovery's meaning changed with the rework below.*
  - **Recoil rework (the user's spec, 2026-11-04, after Delta Force and Tarkov):** no hand-made spray patterns; it's procedural.
    - **Kick:** each round pushes a target up by `up` and sideways by a random amount up to `side` either way. The **first round of a burst kicks 1.4× harder**, so taps need control too; letting go of the trigger for **0.25 s** starts a new burst.
    - **Spring:** the gun chases that target on a damped spring (damping 0.6, so it overshoots each kick a little), at the gun's **snap** (the spring's frequency per second; about 40 lands a kick in a tenth of a second). The target wears off at the gun's **recovery** (a rate per second; after 1/recovery s a third is left). So how hard a gun snaps and how fast it settles are tuned separately. *Until 2026-11-05* recovery ran in full during a burst, so sustained fire leveled off where kick and recovery balance (the AKM at about 7° by round 10); see the climb below. **Once the burst is over** (0.25 s off the trigger, when the next round would be a first round again) recovery is **4× faster**, so the gun comes back down in about half a second instead of hanging (the user's call, same day); quick semi-automatic taps don't count as letting go, so their climb is unchanged.
    - **It keeps climbing, as in Tarkov (the user's call, 2026-11-05, after "I just pull down a little and it stops going up"):** the leveling off came from recovery during the burst, not from the cap. Now, while a burst lasts, the climb wears off at only **10% of the gun's recovery** (`RECOIL.inBurst`; sideways still recovers in full, so the shake doesn't wander), and from the **sixth round** each kick is **half as hard** (`steadyAfter`, `steadyKick`): a hard early kick, then a steady climb that never stops, which the player pulls down. Uncontrolled from standing, a magazine climbs about 21° on the AKM (9° after 5 rounds), 16° on the AUG and 9° on the SMG. The cap went from 20° to 35° so a magazine never reaches it. Letting go still brings it back down in about half a second. First guesses; tune on an iPhone.
    - **Eased (the user's call, 2026-11-11, after "I dump a mag on a guy and only hit him once or twice"):** measured in the sim, an AKM held at the chest without pulling down landed 4 of 30 at 10 m and 1 at 25 m; hit registration itself was fine. In-burst recovery went from 5% to **25%** (`RECOIL.inBurst`) and the late kick from half to **0.35** (`steadyKick`). It still never levels off, but the climb after round 10 is gentle (unscaled AKM: about 6° after 5 rounds, 6.6° at 10, 7.9° at 30). Enemies share the spring, so their long sprays tighten a little too. With it, thumb kick went from 0.6 to **0.75** of a mouse's (`RECOIL.thumb`): at 0.6 a phone held an AKM on a chest at 10 m with no pull-down (29 of 30).
    - **Recoil stays difficult (the user's call, 2026-11-11): the light guns kick harder to match.** After easing the burst, guns with a small kick sat on target with no pull-down (a phone at 10 m landed 84 to 99% of an SMG, AUG or AK-74M mag). The target, aimed at a chest at 10 m with no pull-down: rifles and LMGs land under about a quarter of a mag on a phone, SMGs under half, and on a mouse everything lands under about a fifth. Half a pull-down still holds 10 m, and at 25 m even that lands about a tenth. The kick-up went to: AK-74M 1.75 (from 1.41), AKS-74U 1.85 (1.61), AS VAL 1.7 (1.4), AUG A3 1.7 (1.29), K10 1.55 (1.0), M250 1.95 (1.76), MP5 1.5 (0.92), MP7 1.35 (0.82), MPX 1.4 (0.84), RPK-16 1.85 (1.58) and UMP45 1.85 (1.18). The climb figures quoted with each gun below are from before this change. Measured with a throwaway sim script, not a test.
    - **Stance and movement** scale every kick: standing 1, crouched 0.75, prone 0.5, and ×1.35 moving faster than 0.5 m/s (crawling counts).
    - **Thumbs kick 0.6× (the user's call, 2026-11-08, after "quite hard to aim"):** on a touchscreen or a controller every kick, up and sideways, is ×0.6 (`RECOIL.thumb`); a mouse keeps the full numbers. The guns' numbers came from Tarkov, a mouse game: aimed with iron sights or a red dot, an AKM magazine needed about 115 px of thumb drag to hold down (more through a scope, which is still a separate question), and now about 70. The shape (a hard early kick, then a climb that never stops) and how the guns compare are unchanged. It's a per-player `thumb` flag on the input, so a co-op host applies each player's own. Enemies are unaffected.
    - **All recoil 0.7× (the user's call, 2026-11-10, "reduce recoil across the board by like 30%"):** every gun's kick, up and sideways, for mouse and thumb alike, is ×0.7 (`RECOIL.scale`), on top of the thumb's 0.6×. The guns' numbers in `content/items` stay as scaled from Tarkov, so compare new guns against them as before.
    - **View vs. gun:** from the hip all of recoil is on the view, so the centered hip crosshair stays true. Aimed, the gun and its reticle take about 70% of the first few degrees, then only **10%** of the climb, and the view takes the rest (`gunRecoil`, `RECOIL.aimedShare`, `aimedMax`, `aimedTail`). Decided 2026-11-05 (the user's call, for Tarkov's feel without modeling convergence): a flat 70% put the sights halfway to the top of the screen after an AKM magazine, two-thirds on the SCAR-H and off screen through a 4× scope. A first version stopped the gun at a hard 5°, but then the sights stopped moving at all and recoil seemed to stop with them (the user, on SMGs), so the 10% tail stays: heavier guns end further up, and every round still shows. A magazine now leaves the sights about 20% of the way to the top of the screen on the AKM, and the view climbs about 18°. The result is divided by the scope's zoom, so it's the same distance on the screen. This only changes what's drawn: shots go where the sim says, as they did.
    - **Numbers (up / side / snap / recovery):** AKM 1.8 / 0.7 / 40 / 3, SMG 0.7 / 1 / 45 / 3, pistol 2.4 / 0.9 / 45 / 6, shotgun 6 / 1.8 / 35 / 4, sniper 5 / 1 / 30 / 3, crossbow 0.9 / 0.3 / 30 / 4, grenade launcher 8 / 2 / 25 / 3, flamethrower 0.3 / 0.4 / 20 / 2. First guesses; tune on an iPhone.
    - **Attachments** use the slots there are (optic, muzzle, magazine); grips and stocks would need new slots and mounts on every gun model, so they wait for their own session. The mods for them (`recoilUp`, `recoilSide`, `ergonomics`) already work on any attachment.
    - **Kick angle and steadying (the user's call, same day, after Tarkov's `RecoilAngle` and `RecoilStableIndexShot`):** a gun's optional `angle` (degrees off straight up, + right, at most 45) makes every burst drift that way, on top of the random sideways noise, so its pull can be learnt. Optional `stableAfter` makes the gun stop climbing (and drifting) past that round of a burst: later rounds only top it back up to where that round left it, so it still bounces and shakes. The AKM drifts 8° right and the SMG 12° left; neither steadies early (they level off by themselves around round 10).
    - **Tuning a new gun's recoil:** start from the template (up 1, side 0.4, snap 40, recovery 4). `up` sets how hard it climbs; a magazine's climb is roughly `up × (4 + 0.5 × (rounds − 5))` less a little recovery (`recovery` mostly sets how fast it comes back after the burst), so a fast-firing gun climbs faster for the same `up`. `side` is shake, `angle` is drift. `snap` 30 feels heavy and 45 sharp. `recovery` 2–3 suits automatics, 4–6 semi-automatics. `stableAfter` caps the climb at a chosen round when the balance above lets it go too high.
    - **Softer early kicks, steadier late climb (the user's call, 2026-11-05, after "early shots are too much across the board" and "with smgs I just pull down a little and recoil stops"):** a burst now kicks 1× on its first round and 0.75× on the next four (1.4× and 1× before), then 0.5× as before, and the climb wears off at **5%** of recovery during a burst (10% before, which ate nearly half an SMG's late kicks). The first five rounds climb about 25% less, and the late climb is 25–55% faster. The per-gun climb figures quoted elsewhere in this file are from before this. A magazine now climbs about 23.5° on the AKM (20°), 29° on the SCAR-H (28°), 11.5° on the MP5 (10°) and 9.5° on the K10 (9°). `firstShot` still kicks harder than a later round, but no harder than the gun's `up`.
    - **SMGs and the AS VAL up a little (the user's call, 2026-11-05, after playing the softer curve):** the K10 most, as it should be harder to control, up 0.75 → 1 and side 0.7 → 0.8. The MP5, MPX, MP7 and UMP 45 go up 10% (0.92, 0.84, 0.82, 1.18). The AS VAL goes up 1.19 → 1.4, vertical only. These replace the numbers in their entries, which stay as the scaling from Tarkov's stats.
    - **The sights show exactly where the next round goes**, even mid-burst: they're drawn with the sim's latest recoil rather than eased between ticks (a bug fix, same day: rounds landed up to 0.4° under the reticle at the AKM's peak). They may step slightly at 30 Hz in a burst; the view stays smooth.
    - Enemies still cancel all of their recoil exactly: the spring settles after the tick's shots, so the recoil an enemy aims against is the recoil it fires with.
  - Reticles are drawn on the screen, not in 3D, so they stay sharp and readable at any distance. Their size is in % of the screen height, not angles: a real 2 MOA dot would be a fraction of a pixel on a phone.
