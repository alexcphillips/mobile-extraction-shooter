# Game design: Editors

Part of the [design.md index](../design.md). Record decisions here, in the topic they belong to.

## Editors (admin only, desktop browser)
The editors run on the Mac in a desktop browser and save to JSON files in the repo. The game itself is played on iOS.

**Map editor**
- **Snapping:** adjustable grid snap (coarse for buildings, fine for props, or off). Rotation at any angle, with adjustable angle snapping and **"match the rotation of the selected object"**.
- **Placement:** objects **snap down onto the surface** below (a chair onto the floor, a lamp onto a table). Overlapping props get a **red warning** but are allowed; **buildings can't overlap other buildings**.
- **Prefabs** (cars, trees, trash cans, mailboxes, whole buildings) are placed by reference, so editing a prefab updates every copy. Individual copies can be recolored.
  - **Session 16b (the user's calls, 2026-10-27):** a prefab is **made from a selection**, which is replaced by one copy of it standing where it was, and **edited by entering a placed copy** on a map (there's no prefab-only editor). A change to a prefab that would **break any map is refused at save**, not only the open one. A placed copy can be **unpacked** into a group of its parts, to make a one-off variant without changing every copy (it then isn't a building: no concrete base).
- **Editing safety:** a placed prefab or building is selected and deleted as **one object**, and you can enter it to edit its parts. Full **undo/redo**. **The ground layer is locked** against accidental deletion.
  - **Groups (session 15, the user's call 2026-10-27):** anything can be grouped, and groups nest; a group is moved, turned, copied and deleted as one, and you enter it to edit inside. **Grouping never changes ids**, so quests and patrols that name a marker keep working. The ground layer is the terrain: it can't be selected, so it can't be deleted.
- **Terrain:** raise and lower brushes, water level, and automatic concrete bases under buildings.
  - **Session 16 (the user's calls, 2026-10-27):** a **building** is a prefab that says so, with its footprint (`building.size`); the base fills under that rectangle, and buildings' footprints may not overlap. **An edit that would make buildings overlap is refused** at once (the building shows red while dragged); other things that cut into each other get a red warning and are allowed. **Things standing on the ground move with a brush**; buildings stay put and their base grows or shrinks. Brushes: raise, lower, flatten and smooth.
- **Gameplay markers:** random player spawns, enemy spawns (type, loadout roll, loot weighting), loot containers and spawn points (loot table), extraction points, quest markers, doors and keys, named zones.
  - **Loose loot (2026-11-10):** a loose spot shows the same table-mix fields as a container (a single table shows as a mix of one, written as a mix once changed), without "mix within one container". Selecting placed prefabs and loose spots (one or many) shows a **loot group** name, set on them all as one undo step, and the group's **pick** (stored in the map's `lootGroups`), with how many copies and spots the group has on the map.
- **Navigation overlay:** shows where AI can walk, and **highlights spots where a player can stand but AI can't reach them or shoot them**, so you can fix them while building.
  - **Session 17 (the user's call, 2026-10-28):** a hiding spot is one enemies can't walk to **and** can't see a standing player on from anywhere they can walk within their 70 m sight range. Roofs they can shoot onto aren't flagged. It's worked out when you ask (a button), since it's thousands of rays; walkable and cut-off areas show live. Doors count as unlocked (a key room opens up once unlocked).
- **Easier editing (the user's call, 2026-10-30):** overlaps that are only **joints** (thin on two axes: wall corners, a wall through a slab) don't warn; a **cutaway** hides everything above a height, stepped a floor at a time, to work inside buildings; drags **snap to the faces of things beside them**; a **wall tool** draws walls a corner at a time and **cuts doors and windows** into them, windows sized to the vault rules (Movement). Walls and openings are plain boxes, so the game needs nothing new. **Curves** (the user's call, 2026-11-01, for the Mall's atrium): a curve tool draws curved walls, round floors with a round hole and curved stairs as plain boxes round a circle, the circle clicked being the inside edge; and **cuts a round hole in a floor**, with a railing, placed by number from the floor's middle so nothing is eyeballed. Floor slabs at the same level may overlap without a warning. **Quick floor and wall** buttons (2026-11-01) place thin boxes: a floor 0.2 m thick drawn by clicking two opposite corners (snapping to wall ends, like the wall tool), or, in one click, a 4 m wall at the wall tool's default height and thickness, for blocking out fast. **Named colors** (2026-11-01): the map's settings list its color slots (wall, road…), added and recolored there, and the toolbar's **paint** picks what new shapes, walls, floors and curves are colored, a color or a slot; a shape colored by slot changes with it. A **grid** toggle (H) draws the snapping grid's lines over everything round the camera's target, at the cutaway's height when it's on, every tenth line brighter. Then **a prefab browser with thumbnails**, and **notes**: in any raid on the dev server, N drops a note where you stand; notes are kept per map in `content/notes`, signed with the writer's git name and date, so other developers see them, and show as labels through walls in play-tests and the editor until someone resolves them.
- A **play-test** button.
  - **Session 17 (the user's call, 2026-10-28):** it plays the map **as it is in the editor**, unsaved changes included, in another tab, **offline**: the new-player kit, no server, nothing saved, and a raid saved on the phone left alone. It starts at a random player spawn, a chosen one, or where the camera is looking.

**Item editor**
- Stats for every item type: weapons (stats, ammo type, attachment slots and their mount points, sight line), ammo, attachments (stat changes; for optics, the reticle design and zoom level), medical items (use time, HP pool, what they fix), armor (absorption %, durability), containers (grid size, and **sections**: compartments such as a rig's 1×2 pouches, 2026-11-11: dragged out across the inside grid in the item editor, removed with ×, set by number, or made in one click as columns, rows or one open grid; the preview draws them apart as the inventory does; an **armored** tick box on rigs adds armor level, durability and repair loss, 2026-11-11), keys, quest items.
- A model builder that makes items from simple shapes.
- Grid size in the inventory for every item.
- **Session 18 (the user's calls, 2026-10-28):** the item editor is its own page (`/item-editor`), separate from the map editor. **Saving is refused if the edit breaks anything in content/** that names items (loot tables, loadouts, vendors, quests, door keys, starting kits), as the map editor refuses a broken map. Items can be **made** (blank or as a copy), **not deleted or renamed**. **An item's type can be changed** (2026-11-11, the user's request, for loot that should have been a med): the stats tab's type picker keeps its name, short name, size, value, rarity, view and model, and starts the rest from the new type's blank (`retype`), dropping what only the old type had. **Rigs, armored rigs and backpacks are their own kinds** (2026-11-11, the user's call) in the item list, the type picker and New, though the file still says `container` (which now lists only vaults): a new rig or armored rig starts with four 1×2 pouches and the armored chest rig's model, worn on the chest; changing between them keeps the inside and only sets the slot and armor. **Stats form layout** (2026-11-11, the user's call: the old form was cluttered): a card per group (Item, the type's own groups, Sounds, Picture) with every field a label over its box; short lists are segmented buttons, attachment slots and fitted guns are chips; blanks say what they mean as grayed placeholders instead of "(blank: …)" labels; rarity is colored chips with the one its level or room gives marked **auto** (no separate note or "use …" button); a rig's or backpack's inside is its size, a layout (open, columns, rows, or custom once pouches are drawn), the painter, and exact positions folded under Positions. Models came out of this session into **18b**. A change that can make **players' saved items invalid** (a smaller size, stack, grid, magazine or durability, a slot taken away) **warns** rather than refusing.
- **Session 18b (the user's calls, 2026-10-28):** **every item has a model**, not only those drawn before (the rest start as a box the size of their grid), so the game and the editor have one path. **Inventory icons from the models are not part of 18b.** Models are made of boxes, cylinders and wedges; a gun's parts can be hidden by what's fitted (iron sights under an optic). Worn gear is modeled on the standing body, shown in the editor. The editor previews a gun in first person, from the hip and aimed, with the game's reticle, to check the sight line.
- **IE-1 (planned with the user, 2026-11-09, before the content push):** for guns of many parts and many guns. Several parts are picked at once (shift-click, a box dragged on the view, ⌘A) and moved, turned, duplicated, mirrored, deleted or recolored together as one undo step. Parts can have a **name** and a **group** (editor only; the game ignores them), and the list folds by group, a group's name picking all of it. **Parts copy and paste between items** (⌘C/⌘V) as plain copies; one item never points into another's parts, so changing a rail never silently changes 20 guns. The model tab shows **triangles against a budget** for the item as drawn: **2,000 for a long gun** (the user's call), with smaller ones for pistols, attachments, knives, worn gear and loot, amber when over. A **fits** tab shows every gun with each attachment that fits it, from the side and aimed, to check mounts and sight lines after a gun changes.
- **IE-2 (planned with the user, 2026-11-09):** **flat views** from the side, top and front (keys 1–3, 0 for 3D), the side and top with the barrel to the left as in item pictures, and a **picture to trace** under the side view, kept in the editor on that computer and never in content. **Swatches** of the colors the item already uses and the house item palette (props.md), and an **eyedropper** that gives the picked parts the color of the next part clicked. **Arrow keys** nudge what's picked by the snap step; parts **mirror across y and z** as well as x, as true mirror images. Reticles gain **arcs**, **filled triangles** (chevrons), a **color per shape** (a red center over black lines) and a **repeat** tool that writes plain shapes, so old reticles stay valid.

**Quest editor**
- **2026-11-11 (the user's request: see and edit quest chains, across vendors, without a rename breaking them):** `/quest-editor` shows the chains as a **lane per vendor** (in vendor order, with each lane's quest count and total rep against level IV's), quests left to right in the order they open, an arrow from each quest to those it unlocks (dashed, in the giver's color, when it crosses vendors). Picking or hovering a quest lights up everything before and after it; a search dims the quests that don't name the text, an item or a map. **Dragging from a card's dot onto another** makes that one need it; a loop is refused as it's dropped. The form edits the name, giver, description, requirements and unlocks (chips, × to unlink), objectives (type buttons; a map and its quest markers from dropdowns; items from the loot editor's picker, only those the type allows; count, found in raid, extract spots and find step), and money, rep and item rewards.
  - **Ids are fixed and hidden**, so a name changes freely (the user's call). A new quest's id is **random** (`q-3fa1c2`), not counted up, so a deleted id is never reused and players' leftover progress on it can't wake up on a new quest; objectives' ids too (`o-9b2e`). An objective gets a new id when **what it asks for** changes (type, item, map or marker), so progress on the old ask doesn't count; a count or text change keeps it.
  - **Deleting** a quest passes its requirements on to the quests it unlocked (A → B → C becomes A → C), in one undo step and one save; players' progress on it is ignored, as the game looks progress up from content. New quests are blank, a copy, or "unlocked by" the open one. Saving writes every changed, new and deleted quest at once, refused while `checkQuests` or the marker check finds anything.
  - Vendor files keep their ids (`armorer` is Mara): the editor shows names, and renaming ids needs a profile migration for no gain.

**Sound editor**
- **Session 19 (the user's call, 2026-10-30):** `/sounds` lists every sound by who plays it, edits its layers with a slider for each value (a drag is one undo step and plays the sound when let go), draws its waveform, and plays it centered or from a distance and direction, behind a wall or not, with what that does to it (dB, pan, muffling). New sounds blank or copied; saving like the item editor. The item editor picks an item's sounds, with a button to hear each and a link to tune it.

**Admin page**
- **2026-10-30 (the user's call):** a dev-only **Admin** page off the main menu gives any item into the stash free (one, or a full stack), to test guns, ammo and meds without earning them. Dev server only, like the editors; see [tech-stack.md](../tech-stack.md).
- **Cheats (2026-10-30, the user's call):** the admin page's **Cheats** tab toggles, kept on the device and applied from the next raid (a note says "Admin cheats on" when it starts): **infinite health** (hits land, for markers, but take no HP; no bleeding, no fall damage, no death; armor doesn't wear), **infinite ammo** (firing uses no rounds; an empty gun reloads without spare ammo), **keep inventory on death** (dying or the timer running out keeps everything, as extracting does; a raid that never reports still counts as missing) and **peaceful** (enemies never notice you by sight, sound or being shot, so they patrol on and never fight back; they're still armed and drop their loot). `?peaceful` turns on the same cheat; it used to send enemies in unarmed.
- **More cheats and admin items (2026-10-30, the user's call):** **infinite consumables** (meds and repair kits work as usual but are never used up, in a raid and, from 2026-10-30 at the user's request, in a stash repair between raids too; the server lets a repair mend armor without spending kit points only on the dev server) and **enemy health bars** (a bar over every living enemy within 120 m, through walls, filled by its total HP and labeled with it as current/max, e.g. 185/370, red under 35%). The admin page's **Admin** tab gives admin-only items: the **Scav spawner** and **Boss spawner**, pistol-held guns (they go in the sidearm slot) whose shot puts an enemy of that type where it lands, 0.6 m short of it, facing you, with its type's loadout and loot. **The Boss spawner spawns Jug** (the user's call, 2026-11-08; it was the `boss-scav` "Yard boss"), with Jug's limbs and voice; spawning the current map's boss is a roadmap to-do. Their shots harm no one and use no rounds, and auto-fire never pulls their trigger (the new enemy stands under the crosshair, so it would spawn them in a chain). Spawned enemies stay in a saved raid. Worth pairing with peaceful.
- **Admin backpack (2026-10-31, the user's request: "basically just the backpack item but really big", for testing):** `admin-backpack`, on the admin page's **Admin** tab: the large backpack (4×4, worn in the backpack slot) holding an **8 × 15 grid** (120 cells; the large one holds 30), drawn bigger and red so it's never mistaken for a real one. Kept 8 wide so the inventory screen's gear column still fits beside the loot on a phone; it scrolls. Value 0, so it can't be sold; in no loot table, loadout or vendor.
- **A page per mode (2026-11-04, the user's request):** the admin page shows only what the mode whose menu opened it uses. From **Extraction** it has the item tabs and every cheat; **the item tabs leave out zombies-only items** (the user's call, 2026-11-04): the death machine, Gale Cannon and Gale charge, Ion Blaster and Ion bolt, and zombie claws (any mode's box guns, minigun and claws worth nothing to sell, and those guns' own ammo). From **Zombies** it has the **Zombies** tab (infinite points, guns to start with) and a **Cheats** tab without infinite consumables or keep inventory (zombies has no meds and keeps nothing), no item tabs and no Stash button, as nothing from the stash goes in. The cheats themselves are shared: turning one on in either mode turns it on in both.
