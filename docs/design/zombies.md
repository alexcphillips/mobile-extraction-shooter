# Game design: Zombies

Part of the [design.md index](../design.md). Record decisions here, in the topic they belong to.

## Zombies (2026-11-04, the user's request and calls)
A separate game mode, round-based survival in the style of other wave shooters' zombie modes, alongside the extraction game. PvE only.
- **Separate from the stash:** everyone starts with the mode's fixed kit (a pistol, the knife, a chest rig and 9 mm); nothing from the stash goes in and nothing comes out, so it can't touch the extraction economy. **There's no inventory in it** (the bag button is gone; changed after the first play-test). It needs no server: it starts from the Zombies menu's **Start game** (solo only; it's disabled in a party) or `?zombies`, and the results screen shows the round reached, kills, points left and what hit you.
- **One health bar, not limbs (after the first play-test):** 150 HP, no broken limbs, no bleeding, no hit-zone multipliers. A zombie's claws take 50, so three hits kill. It refills (60 HP/s) once you've gone 3 s without being hurt.
- **Rounds are endless:** each has more zombies (6, then 3 more a round) and tougher ones (half a player's health in round 1, +15% a round). **They never get faster** (the user's call, 2026-11-04: running zombies came out; every zombie walks). At most 20 are alive at once; they appear one every 2 s, and there's a 10 s break between rounds (and before the first). You play until you die.
- **Zombies come from outside, through barricaded windows (after the first play-test):** they appear in the fenced yard round the building, walk up the paths to their window, tear its planks off one at a time (1.5 s each), then climb in over the sill and hunt you. A spawn names its window; it's used only while a living player can walk to that window's inside, so buying the door opens up the back room's windows. Players can never climb out. **Shots and claws go through a window:** you can shoot zombies through the planks, and a zombie at the window claws you if you stand in reach, so rebuilding while one is tearing at it costs health.
- **Barricades:** 6 planks a window. **Hold use** at a window short of planks and they go back up one at a time (0.8 s each); a full window takes no more. Each plank pays 10 points, up to 500 a player a round, so it can't be farmed.
- Inside, zombies are melee rushers: they always know where you are, take the shortest way to the nearest player they can walk to, and claw from close up. No guns, no hiding. Their bodies go 4 s after they die and have nothing to loot.
- **Points:** 500 to start, +10 for a hit (once per zombie per tick, so a shotgun blast is one hit), +60 a kill, +100 a knife kill, +10 a plank, all doubled under double points.
- **What points buy:** a **door** (750 in the first map), which **disappears when bought, leaving the doorway** (as in other zombie modes; changed after the first play-test, when it swung open); a **couch** (1000) blocking a stairway, which disappears the same way; a **wall gun** (SMG 1000, shotgun 1200, rifle 1500, sniper rifle 2000 upstairs; the SV-98 since 2026-11-05), drawn as its model hung on the wall, or **its ammo by buying it again while you carry it**, at a fraction of its price (half unless the map says otherwise); and the **mystery box** (950) for a random gun from the mode's list, one you don't carry if it can. **The ammo refill station was taken out** (the user's call, 2026-11-04): buy a wall gun again, or find a max ammo. Buying a gun or ammo fills its magazine and 4 spare magazines into pockets and rig, of the gun's own round unless the mode names another for it (`ammo`, 2026-11-05: the shotgun loads **dragon's breath**, the zombies-only round). A rifle goes into an empty primary slot, else in place of the primary in hand; a pistol replaces the sidearm. Nothing to fill costs nothing, and no new gun while you hold the death machine. **The box's gun hovers over it (the user's call, 2026-11-04):** paying shows the gun it gave floating above the box for about 7 seconds, blinking in its last two; use the box again while it's there to take it (free, as it's paid for), or it goes and the points are spent. Only the player who paid can take it, and the box can't be used again until it's taken or gone. No spin before it shows (could come later).
- **The Ion Blaster (zombies only, the user's request, 2026-11-04):** an original ray gun, held like a pistol (it takes the sidearm slot): fast glowing bolts that burst on whatever they hit, killing zombies close by. 20 a magazine. From the mystery box, about 1 spin in 10. **A player's own blasts never hurt players in zombies mode**, so it (and the grenade launcher) can be fired at zombies at your feet.
- **Power-ups (2026-11-04, the user's request):** a zombie a player kills drops one 6% of the time (raised from 3%, the user's call, 2026-11-04), at most 4 a round. It floats where the zombie fell for 25 s, blinking near the end, and the first player to walk over it takes it. **Insta-kill** (30 s: any hit on a zombie kills it), **double points** (30 s), **max ammo** (every player's spare rounds filled, not what's loaded), **carpenter** (every window boarded up again, +200 points) and the **death machine** (a minigun in your hands for 30 s that never runs out; what was in your first gun slot comes back after; **it can't be put away** (the user's call, 2026-11-04): no switching to another gun or the knife until it ends). Insta-kill and double points are everyone's; their seconds left show beside the round. **All five are equally likely** (the user's call, 2026-11-04: the death machine was rarer, at 0.6 of the others' weight, and hardly ever turned up in play).
- **The Gale Cannon (zombies only, the user's request):** an original wonder weapon, a blast gun: each shot kills every zombie within 14 m and 35° either side that nothing solid hides, and flings them away. 2 shots a magazine. Only from the mystery box, about 1 spin in 40; in no loot table, vendor or loadout, and worth nothing to sell.
- **The first map,** *Two Rooms:* a start room with the SMG, and a back room behind a 750-point door with the rifle, the shotgun and the mystery box. Four barricaded windows in each room, each with its own spawn and dirt path out in the fenced yard. **An upper floor** (2026-11-04) over both, with the sniper rifle: reached by a **spiral staircase** in the start room's corner, behind a couch, or by **straight stairs** up the back room's side of the middle wall, behind another. It has no windows, so zombies come up the stairs. The couches lie **tipped over on their backs**, a little askew, across the stairways (the user's call, 2026-11-04), as if knocked over to block them.
- **Admin tools (dev builds only, the user's request, 2026-11-04):** the admin page's **Zombies** tab has **infinite points** (doors, couches, wall guns and the box cost nothing; the HUD shows ∞), **every kill drops a power-up** (the user's call, 2026-11-04: each zombie you kill drops one, past the 4-a-round cap, so power-ups can be tested quickly) and guns to **start every zombies game with** (any box gun or the minigun, zombies-only ones like the Ion Blaster listed first; they go in as bought ones do). In a game, an **Admin** button (or K) opens a panel to give yourself any power-up, +1,000 or +10,000 points, any of those guns, or kill every zombie. Solo only for now.
- **Later, not yet:** co-op (downed and revive, shared or own points), perks, power-ups, the box's spin.
