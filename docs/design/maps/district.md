# Map: District

Part of the [maps index](../raids-and-maps.md#maps). Rules shared by every map are in [raids-and-maps.md](../raids-and-maps.md). Record this map's decisions here. Moved here from raids-and-maps.md on 2026-11-12 (MK-1), word for word.

## Summary
- **What:** a new map, "Northpoint v2" while planning: a suburban commercial district on a hill with the mall at its center. Story: an evacuation point that was overrun.
- **Size and shape:** an irregular play edge inside 480 × 380 m bounds, about 136k m² of play (DS-1 take 3, with the station): a wooded escarpment cuts off the north-east, a highway embankment runs down the east, a sunken rail cut runs along the south-west. The edge is the zone `play-area` in the layout file.
- **Areas:** nine, unevenly spaced, not a ring round the mall (the user's call, 2026-11-12); the in-between is about 70% of the map and designed ground.
- **Aims:** no line across a fighting space much over 30 m; every place has a job; landmarks to orient by; several ways into every building.
- **Status:** bones graybox built (DS-2, 2026-11-12), waiting for the user to walk it (DS-3). **District will replace Northpoint (the user's call, 2026-11-12).** DS-1's sketch (take 3 plus the user's changes) is approved provisionally (https://claude.ai/artifact/DscuBdQ2HnzDhaTT9PPiES); the real approval is at DS-3, walking it. Northpoint is not touched.
- **Generator:** `scripts/gen-district.mjs` (ground, roads, the sunken routes, edges, labels, the raid) and `scripts/district-build.mjs` (the areas as boxes), building from the layout file `content/layouts/district.json` (edit it at `/layout?map=district`, then re-run) with the map kit (MK-6). `--report` prints steep ground, road grades, wall stairs and road decks. `scripts/district-probe.mjs` compiles the map and asks the nav which places join the test spawn (`--drain` piece by piece).
- **Files:** `content/maps/district.json`, `content/raids/district.json` (`dev: true`: in dev builds' raid list only, tagged test); map id `district`.

## Notes
- **District (2026-11-12, the user's call; "Northpoint v2" while planning, named District by the user; planning only, roadmap → District, map id `district`):** a new map built from scratch by generator script, not a fix of Northpoint, which stays untouched until v2 plays. Why: the court's void (about 59 × 34 m, a 68 m diagonal) is inside the AI's 70 m sight, so one peek shows all of it and the middle is skipped; the AI has no reason to be anywhere (only the boss gives tension); the upper loops have no risk and don't tell you where you are; the furniture store's side area and the department store are empty and open; the stores have no personality; it's symmetrical. Keeps the aims: a center, a flat-pack furniture store, a warehouse and loading bay, an upper walkway, key stores. **Story: an evacuation point that was overrun** (Claude's default, the user agreed): it gives one-off geometry (checkpoint, barricades, field hospital, damage) that breaks lines and symmetry and gives the AI places to be. **Aims for the bones:** no line across a fighting space much over 30 m (Silver Creek's lanes break at 21–27 m); every place does a job that shapes it, as on Silver Creek; the upper level isn't a featureless loop; landmarks you can orient by; no area is a collection of plain rectangles (silhouettes, elevation, several ways into every building).
- **A district, not an enclosed mall (the user's call, 2026-11-12):** a suburban commercial center on a **hill**. The mall straddles the slope: its front door at street level on the low side, its upper floor and **roof terrace** opening onto the high side. The mall is the central landmark and the richest loot, but it must not divide the map: you go **around** it by either flank, **over** it by the high side, **through** its public floor, or **behind** its storefronts by the service corridors, and none of them is required. The old "inside only, no car park" rule was Northpoint's and doesn't apply here.
- **Size: about 350 × 350 m to start (Claude's estimate, not measured; 650 × 700 was considered and judged far too empty for 70 m AI sight and 120 m fog)**, about seven times Silver Creek's area, a raid of about 25 minutes. The generator keeps the layout in one table so the size changes after the bones review (roadmap DS-3).
- **The nine areas (Claude's picks, the user to confirm in DS-1):** **the mall** (the user's department store spec as the corner anchor, the cinema wing, the court with an indoor garden, the helicopter crash, bridges across the void, the service network with locked security doors, breakable access points and staff stairs, the roof terrace); **the parking garage** (above ground, 4–5 decks, a landmark; its top deck meets the mall's upper floor by a **glass connector bridge**, the quick, exposed way in); **the plaza** on the high side (open-air promenade, a restaurant court of three kitchens and a service alley, the jeweler, bank branch, phone repair); **the hotel** on the high side (the military's command post during the evacuation and **the boss's ground**, a second hotspot away from the mall; lobby, kitchen and two floors full depth, the rest of the tower a facade); **the service quarter** on a flank (docks, the mall's warehouse as its own building, a substation, angled container stacks like Northpoint's Cans group); **the flat-pack furniture store** standing alone on the other flank; **the construction site** beside it (a half-built office block, a tower crane as the second landmark); **the strip row and gas station** on the low side (grocery, pharmacy with a dispensary key room, sporting goods, convenience store; dentist and travel agency as one-room or facade fronts); **the surface lot** on the low side (the convoy wreck and armored vehicle as quest ground, the abandoned landing zone; military crates are the reason to cross it).
- **Saved for later maps:** a hospital (its own map), a casino, nightclub and aquarium (a downtown or waterfront map), botanical gardens, the hardware store with garden center, a separate furniture showroom, a standalone electronics store, a business center.
- **The in-between (about 60% of the map) is designed ground, not filler, with a job like everything else:** the evacuation jam (rows of abandoned cars on the exit road), the slope's terraces, retaining walls and stair cascades, a pedestrian underpass under the ring road, a drainage culvert as a sunken route round the lot, service roads and alleys behind each row, small structures (bus shelter, kiosks, drive-through, car wash, billboards, cell tower), landscaping (tree islands, hedges as concealment, berms), and evacuation leftovers (barrier lines, sandbag posts, burnt cars). **Rules:** no more than 40 m of open ground (nothing on it, neither cover nor concealment) on any crossing, except the named long crossings at the rich places (level-design.md principle 11: the parade ground to the hotel, the lot-to-furniture flats), pieces every 15–25 m on the routes people use; every gap has a fast exposed way and a slower covered one; every POI has at least three ways in from different connectors; landmarks (garage, crane, hotel tower, the helicopter's tail on the mall roof) are visible from the in-between.
- **Building depths:** full (every room), partial (one or two rooms) and facade (landmark only), with shared generator kits (storefronts, office floors) rather than one-offs.
