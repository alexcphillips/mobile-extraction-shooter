# Map: Silver Creek

Part of the [maps index](../raids-and-maps.md#maps). Rules shared by every map are in [raids-and-maps.md](../raids-and-maps.md). Record this map's decisions here. Moved here from raids-and-maps.md on 2026-11-12 (MK-1), word for word.

## Summary
- **What:** the starter map (raid order 1, tag `starter`, 20-minute raids). A sawmill company town in a valley, after the feel of Zero Dam, an original layout.
- **Size:** play area x 0–320, z 0–150 m (north is −z); valley floor y 0, ridge to the north; terrain 370 × 200 m at 2 m cells.
- **Shape:** a Customs-style rectangle: players spawn at both ends and leave by the far end; five river crossings; three landmarks past the fog; trees kept to the edges.
- **Areas:** west end (company houses, store, office, fire lookout), the sawmill core and pond, the middle and the ridge (log deck, timber yard, bridge, weir ford), the east end (rail yard, engine shed, water tower, depot).
- **Enemies:** fewer and worse-equipped, not dumber (design/ai-scavs-pmcs-bosses.md → Silver Creek; the foreman is its boss).
- **Status:** v1 built (MT-2 to MT-12), not played yet. Open: MT-13 (conditional extracts). Sketch: https://claude.ai/artifact/9v38f2y7U7tuf5cdKBxc84.
- **Generator:** `node scripts/gen-silver-creek.mjs [--dry-run] [--reclaim <key>|all]`, building from the map kit (`scripts/mapkit/`: nodes, terrain grid, building shells, fences), `-west`, `-middle`, `-east` (areas), `-ai` (enemies) and `-quests` (markers). Re-running keeps hand edits: a group edited by hand stays as it is and is listed (MK-2, tech/editors.md → Generated maps); the raid file is never overwritten.
- **Files:** `content/maps/silver-creek.json`, `content/raids/silver-creek.json`.

## Notes
- **Silver Creek, a planned starter map (2026-11-09, idea only, nothing built; "Mill Town" while planning, named by the user's pick of Claude's suggestion):** Northpoint is the hard map, so the next map is a beginner one, after the feel of Delta Force's Zero Dam (an original layout, not a copy). A port was considered first and kept for a later map: its open quays give long flat sightlines that new players can't survive against the AI, and it wants systems we don't have yet (a boat extract, water that slows you, paid extracts). Silver Creek is a sawmill company town in a valley (the mill is the Silver Creek Lumber Co.), a rectangle of about 320 × 150 m with a 20-minute raid, laid out like Tarkov's Customs: **players spawn at both ends and leave by the far end** (the user's call), using per-spawn extractions. Trees stay on the ridge, the south edge and a few small groups used as cover, because a forest hides landmarks and loses players (the user's point). The creek (a river in size) runs north to south through the middle with five crossings: log jam, log boom over the mill pond, the sawmill itself (the hot core), road bridge, weir ford. A mill race tunnel under the mill bypasses the core. There are three landmarks, drawn past the fog so you always know which end you face: fire lookout on the ridge (west; the steeple first planned was dropped, 2026-11-10: the user wants no churches in the game), teepee burner (center), water tower (east). Perches are kept within about 60 m of AI positions or reached by stairs, because the AI sees only 70 m and can't climb ladders, so a distant perch would be a free sniper spot. The whiteboard sketch is at https://claude.ai/artifact/9v38f2y7U7tuf5cdKBxc84.
- **Agreed with the user the same day:** deep water (over about 1.2 m) blocks movement, so the creek is crossed only at the crossings. Easier because of **how many enemies there are and what they carry, not dumber AI** (no mercy still holds): fewer, poorer-geared scavs at the two ends, 2–4 PMCs (Northpoint 3–6), the strong ones around the mill. First in the map list with a "starter" tag; Northpoint stays open to everyone (no level lock; changed 2026-11-11, see below). 3–5 starter quests on Silver Creek that teach the crossings, landmarks and tunnel. Claude generates the graybox by script and the user reshapes it in the editor. Claude's defaults, to check in MT-3: 20-minute raids, an overcast autumn look, party members spawn together.
- **The graybox (MT-2, 2026-11-09, `scripts/gen-silver-creek.mjs`):** play area x 0–320, z 0–150 (north is −z), the valley floor at y 0 and the ridge rising to 7 m at the north edge, its trail on a level bench at z 17–23 (2.9 m).
