# Map: Silver Creek

Part of the [maps index](../raids-and-maps.md#maps). Rules shared by every map are in [raids-and-maps.md](../raids-and-maps.md). This file says what the map is now and why; the history is in git and roadmap-done.md. "The user's call" marks decisions not to reverse without asking; everything else is Claude's and easy to change.

## Summary
- **What:** the starter map (raid order 1, tag `starter`, 20-minute raids, overcast autumn). A sawmill company town in a valley (the Silver Creek Lumber Co.), after the feel of Delta Force's Zero Dam, an original layout.
- **Size:** play area x 0–320, z 0–150 m (north is −z); valley floor y 0, ridge to the north; terrain 370 × 200 m at 2 m cells.
- **Shape (the user's call):** a Customs-style rectangle. Players spawn at both ends and leave by the far end (per-spawn extractions); party members spawn together.
- **Spawns and extracts (2026-11-17, the user's calls, with a PvP mode in mind one day).** At 320 × 150 m the map takes **four parties** in PvP. **Eight spawn points** round the edge (`west-office`, `west-lot`, `north-lookout`, `south-bank`, `north-jam`, `east-ridge`, `east-tower`, `east-yard`); a PvP raid would pick four of them at least about 100 m apart, so nobody learns the rush angles (that picking is PvP logic, not built). PvE picks one at random, so a party now sometimes starts on the ridge above the jam or on the south bank by the powerhouse, nearer the mill: their far-end extracts are about 0:50–1:03 away against about 1:35 from the ends. Every spawn starts behind hard cover (group "Spawn cover") with a way along the edge and a way in toward the mill, at least 29 m from every extract, and no neighbor can see it (checked by raycast at eye height; blockers on the west and east edge strips, a lineside signal hut north of the water tower's field). **Extracts:** the four stay at the ends (the user's pick over adding or moving them); each spawn gets the **far end's** two (west of the creek: rail tunnel and highway east; east of it: highway west and ridge west). The user's note was "the opposite half"; on this long east–west map that's the far end, since the north–south halves would let a north-west spawn walk 100 m down the west edge to the south-west extract. **A contested central extract** comes with MT-13: the paid log truck by the road bridge (conditional extracts need code). **Enemy clearance** now counts from the spawns used in the raid, not every spawn point (the user's call; tech in sim/waves.ts startEnemies): two AI wave spawns moved off the new points (`sc-edge-sc` 150,147 → 105,148; `sc-edge-n` 185,8 → 212,6).
- **The west extracts made places (2026-11-17):** the highway leaves by the **company gate** (posts and an arch with the company's board over the road at x 9.6, a solid gatehouse north of it, the boom left raised, two jersey barriers staggered across the last stretch with a lane past each); the ridge trail by its **trailhead** (a solid map board on posts at the bench's north edge, a log bench, the gate bar fallen across the trail's edge, a boulder on the open bench between the zone and the trail's first pile). The east two were places already (the roadworks, the tunnel portal).
- **Areas, west to east:** the company town (houses, store, office, fire lookout), the middle (log deck, timber yard, crossings, powerhouse), the sawmill core (saw hall, pond, kilns, burner, substation), the ridge along the north, the rail yard and east end (engine shed and loco shop, water tower, bunkhouse, depot).
- **Status:** v1 built, not played yet. Open: MT-13 (conditional extracts), loot placement (the user's), the end-of-map electrical spots.
- **Sketch:** https://claude.ai/artifact/9v38f2y7U7tuf5cdKBxc84

## Rules for this map
- **Easier through numbers and gear, not dumber AI** (no mercy still holds): fewer, poorer-geared scavs at the ends, the strong ones round the mill. AI detail: design/ai-scavs-pmcs-bosses.md → Silver Creek.
- **Trees at the edges (the user's call):** on the ridge, the south edge and a few cover copses, because a forest hides landmarks and loses players. Leaves are `foliage`: they block sight (the AI's too) but not shots, grenades or sound.
- **Three landmarks drawn past the fog**, so you always know which end you face: the fire lookout (west), the teepee burner (center), the water tower (east). **No churches** (the user's call).
- **Perches within about 60 m of AI ground or reached by stairs:** the AI sees 70 m and can't climb ladders, so a distant ladder perch would be a free sniper spot. Every perch is seen from AI ground 4–7 m away at the nearest.
- **Water (the user's call):** the creek and pond are 1 m deep (`WADE`), waded anywhere at 70% speed, by the AI too. The crossings are the fast, dry ways over. Banks fall at 35° (`BANK`) to below the deep line, so wading out never traps you.
- **Key rooms have one way in**, the locked door (the user's call); a separate way out may come later.
- **The open field by the burner stays open** (the user's call).
- **Claude builds places, the user places loot**, spawns and extractions' final spots.

## Water and crossings
- The creek runs north to south through the middle. Pond and upper creek at −0.5, below the mill dam at −2; the race is a wadeable ditch into the saw hall's north-west corner.
- **Mill dam:** a 2.2 m concrete wall across the creek at z 69.5, too high to climb, so it isn't a crossing.
- **Five crossings:** the **log jam** (logs across the ravine at trail height; its tangle is drawn only, so nobody climbs onto it from the water), the **log boom** over the pond (with the boom man's shack on a raft mid-pond, doors at opposite ends, breaking the boom's line), the **saw hall** (the hot core), the **road bridge** (22 m, waist rails, posts, lamps; a loaded log truck west and a tractor unit east), the **weir ford** (0.6 m deep over 8 m, two boulders as waist cover, a gauge post, a fallen tree).
- **Mill race tunnel:** x 116–216, z 104–108, floor at −6, stairs at both ends (the AI uses them) and a player-only ladder hatch on the east bank. Eight staggered pieces of cover (1.6 m of its 3 m width, 1.7 m high) keep its longest line to about 23 m. Its roof under the creek follows the banks (a 2 m ramp per terrain cell) and its cover can't be jumped through (tech/shared-sim.md). It bypasses the core.
