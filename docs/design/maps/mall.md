## Notes
- **The Mall (2026-10-30, the user's design):** **Deleted 2026-11-09 (the user's call): its map, raid and notes are gone; Northpoint took its place.** Kept here as a record. It was a second map, a two-storey mall (112 × 48 m) in a fenced car park, with a roof.
- **Ground floor:** the west half is **Fjordhem**, a flat-pack furniture store in blue and yellow (a showroom of 16 room sets along aisles marked with arrows, a café, a market hall of racking and checkouts); the east half is **Marlowe's**, a department store (cosmetics, clothes, home, jewelry counter, shoes, a stockroom and the manager's office). A lobby between them has the main entrance, a fountain and two escalators up.
- **Upper floor:** a concourse with shops on both sides and service corridors behind them: **Lockstock Arms** (the gun store, with the boss, over Fjordhem at the west end), tech shops (Voltix, Pixel Pit, Byte Bros, Glint Mobile, Megavolt, Snap Cameras, Pulse Audio), **Brickburg** (a brick-toy store), a pharmacy, clothes and books, the security office, and the **food court** at the east end. Its **Asian Bear To-Go** has a vault behind the kitchen, locked with the **Bear vault key**, holding jewelry (rings, necklaces, a jade dragon, gold bars).
- **The roof** is reached only by the **emergency stairs** at the west end (ground → upper → roof), whose roof door is locked: the **Roof access key** lies in one random store on the east side each raid (a `oneOf` set of 8 spots). The **Rooftop helipad** is an extraction, guarded by a scav who can't come down until the door is unlocked. The other extractions are the **Bus stop** (south gate) and the **Service road** (north, past the loading dock). The vault key is in one of three offices (security, Fjordhem's stockroom, Marlowe's).
- Names are made up; real brands are only echoed in colors.
- **The Bear vault key was deleted too (2026-11-09, the user's call)**, with its `vault-key` loot table and its Fence listing. The `documents` table (filing cabinets, filing drawers, briefcases, suitcases), which held only that key, now holds **Trade documents**, a plain common loot item (1 × 1, 2,500).
