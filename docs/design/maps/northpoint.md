# Map: Northpoint

Part of the [maps index](../raids-and-maps.md#maps). Rules shared by every map are in [raids-and-maps.md](../raids-and-maps.md). Record this map's decisions here. Moved here from raids-and-maps.md on 2026-11-12 (MK-1), word for word.

## Summary
- **What:** the hard map (raid order 2, level 2 and up, 20-minute raids). An enclosed two-floor mall left mid-day, plus its fenced loading yard. Inside only for v1.
- **Size:** terrain about 300 × 300 m at 2 m cells; the mall and yard take the middle.
- **Areas:** the atrium and its upper ring, the flat-pack furniture store (about 80 × 82 m), the department store (about 44 × 80 m), the tech store, the south and north stores, laser tag, the bank vault, the warehouse (about 70 × 40 m), the loading yard with the admin building.
- **Enemies:** about 12 scavs, PMCs 3–6, Jug in 40% of raids (design/ai-scavs-pmcs-bosses.md → PMCs and Jug).
- **Status:** playable. Extractions are still test placements; player spawns are deliberate; loot comes last. District may replace it once District plays.
- **Generator:** none; built by hand in the map editor.
- **Files:** `content/maps/northpoint.json`, `content/raids/northpoint.json`.

## Notes

### Furniture store
- **Northpoint furniture store layout (2026-11-06, the user's call, still blocked out in plain walls and `-temp` cubes):** a second, separate store from Fjordhem, on the Northpoint map.
- **Size (2026-11-06, the user's call):** a full-size anchor, about 80 × 82 m, with its roof flush with the second floor at 6.2 m so that atrium paths can run out over it (2026-11-06), nearly double the first blockout. It grew north, past the mall's front line, so it reads as a big box beside the mall.
- **Flow:** entrance portal in the storefront wall, facing the atrium → entrance hall → showrooms, walked east along the foyer and north up aisle 2 → market hall → tall self-serve warehouse → checkouts → exit on the west wall of the north block, straight out to the front of the mall. Checkouts are never in front of the entrance. Colors echo the real thing; the name will be made up.
- **Showrooms finished (2026-11-06):** 19 room sets, each with its own floor, one painted wall and furniture, no two alike. A1 kitchen wall, B1 storage wall, A2 bedroom, A3 dining room, B2 kids' room, B3 open-front living room, B4 office, and C1, C2 and C3 as small homes. One floor only (the user's call), and long one-piece gondolas and racks in the market and warehouse so they stay cheap. No loot yet.
- **Density (2026-11-06, the user's note):** the big spaces should feel as full as the showrooms, so nothing reads as bare floor: small room sets line the entrance hall, and the market and warehouse are filled with bins, stacks, plants and signs. There are no arrows on the floor; signs do that job.
- **Staff area (2026-11-06, the user's call, replacing the restaurant):** the strip beside the entrance hall is back-of-house, behind a staff-only door off the passage to the market. Going in: lockers and break room, security office, manager's office, stock and returns, then the cash room, a dead end behind its own door. Offset dividers mean you can't see along it. A door from stock and returns leads into the warehouse, about 132 m to the exit against about 212 m the long way. The cash room door takes a **Staff keycard** (3 uses), which lies on one of three desks each raid (security, manager, break room), a `oneOf` set. Its two safes are containers; their table is a placeholder until the loot pass.
- **Fix lanes with partitions, not by moving rooms:** the blocks stay where they are and the `showroom-partitions` group joins them and adds offset stubs (S-bends) in the two long aisles. A partition is cheap and reversible; squishing rooms keeps the lanes straight and moves 100+ wall pieces.
- **The entrance hall must stay sealed from the old street.** Its north wall runs unbroken from the storefront to A2's corner, so people go round the showrooms. With a gap there, the showrooms get skipped.
- **Shortcuts are meant to exist:** the full trip from entrance to exit is about 210 m, cutting through A3 is shorter, and the key door and goods shutter both land near the start of the showrooms, about 182 m from the exit.
