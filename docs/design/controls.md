# Game design: Controls and platform behavior

Part of the [design.md index](../design.md). Record decisions here, in the topic they belong to.

## Controls and platform behavior
- A standard twin-stick touch layout. **The layout can be adjusted in settings and is saved.**
- **Button layouts (UX-4, 2026-11-08):** settings → Buttons keeps **3 saved layouts**, one equipped (used in raids); "Edit layout N" opens the editor on the equipped one. Each layout has its own places, sizes and **hidden buttons**: the editor's Hide/Show takes a button off the HUD (it shows faded while editing, so it can come back). **Fire, use and bag can't be hidden**, since on touch nothing else fires, loots or opens the bag. Put out and detonate can be hidden, though only they do what they do (the user's call, 2026-11-08). **Share codes:** each layout has a code (`MES1-` then base64url, from about 8 characters for the default layout to about 140 for every button moved) to copy and paste into a chat; "Paste a code" loads one into the equipped layout, asking first if that layout isn't the default. No server: positions are fractions of the screen, so a code fits any phone. A code has a check byte, so a mistyped or cut-off one is refused, and a code from a newer build skips the buttons this one doesn't know. The format is `hud/layout-code.ts`; control numbers there are append-only. **Settings:** `layouts` (3 slots of `placements` and `hidden`) and `equippedLayout` replace `layout`; an old `layout` becomes slot 1 when loaded, with no format bump, so every other field still falls back on its own. Layouts are named Layout 1–3, with no custom names (the user's call, 2026-11-08).
- **Aim and fire behavior** are chosen in settings (hold vs. toggle to aim, fire options). Default: hold, with automatic fire.
  - **"Automatic fire" means auto-fire on target** (clarified 2026-10-26, session 5): the gun fires by itself while the crosshair is on an enemy, like CoD Mobile's simple mode. The other option is "fire button only". The fire button works in both modes.
  - Session 6: "on an enemy" means the crosshair ray (with recoil) hits a living character's hit zone within the gun's range, with no wall in the way. With a semi-automatic gun, auto-fire pulls the trigger as fast as the gun allows.
- **Aim assist:** aim slows slightly when passing over an enemy. It's a setting, defaulting to low. Session 6: look speed × 0.8 (low), 0.6 (medium) or 0.45 (high) while the crosshair is on an enemy. **It never slows pulling down against recoil (the user's call, 2026-11-08):** during a burst (until the trigger has been let go 0.25 s, so auto-fire and semi taps count) it slows only turning sideways; up and down stay at full speed. Before, low assist made an aimed AKM magazine need about 145 px of drag instead of 115.
- **Touch layout (session 5):** the move stick floats where the left thumb lands on the left half; dragging on the right half looks. **Near-misses press the button (2026-11-11):** a touch within 12px of a shown button's edge presses the closest one instead of starting the stick or a look drag, so a missed button on the left doesn't walk you. **The fire button also looks:** dragging the thumb on it turns the view, so you can track a target while shooting. Buttons: fire (it says "knife" with the knife out), aim, reload, use (interact), bag, jump, crouch (toggle, or hold: a setting since 2026-11-08), the four hand slots and heal (session 10b, replacing quick slots 1–4).
- **Sprint (decided 2026-10-26, session 5):** pushing the stick to its rim (forwards) sprints. Dragging on up into the "sprint" zone above the stick **locks sprinting**, so the thumb can let go; with a thumb on the stick while locked, left and right steer. **It unlocks** when you pull the stick back, tap the left half, aim, crouch, lean, or jump off a ladder. Aiming stops sprinting. **Whichever came last wins** (2026-11-10, the user's call): starting to sprint stands you up straight (crouch, prone and lean off); crouching, going prone or leaning mid-sprint stops the sprint until sprint is let go or you stand straight again.
- **Desktop works too** (the user's wish, 2026-10-26): every touch action has a keyboard and mouse equivalent, so the game can be played and tested at a desk.
  - **Proper PC controls (the user's request, 2026-10-30):** on a computer the game plays like a PC shooter, not a phone game clicked with a mouse. **The mouse is captured** (pointer lock): moving it looks, **left click fires, right click aims** (hold or toggle, as the aim setting says; Q follows it too). WASD, Shift sprint, Space jump, C crouch (toggle or held, as the crouch setting says), R reload, **E use** (take, open, loot, investigate), I or Tab the bag, 1–4 the hand slots, H heal (holding H opens its picker, as the touch button does; Shift+H was dropped on 2026-10-30 because Shift is sprint, so healing on the run opened the picker); F and Q still fire and aim. **The touch buttons are hidden**: no stick, fire, aim, reload, use, bag, jump or crouch. The hand slots and heal button stay as a read-out (not clickable). With no use button, **the prompt under the crosshair says what E does** ("E Loot Supply crate", "E Take Bandage", "E Open door"). **Rebound 2026-11-01 for leaning (the user's call):** Q and E lean, **use moves to F** (Tarkov's key; the prompt says F now), **Z aims** (hold or toggle, for a trackpad, where right click is a two-finger click), and **F no longer fires** (a click fires).
  - **No "click to play" (the user's call, 2026-10-30, replacing the overlay):** a raid starts straight in, capturing the mouse from the click that started it if the browser allows, otherwise from your first click in the game (which also fires). **Esc during play opens settings** (Resume captures the mouse again); **Esc doesn't close the inventory, a loot screen or the heal picker** (it does nothing there): browsers never capture the mouse again from an Esc press, so you'd come back unable to turn until you clicked. Tab or Close closes the inventory and the mouse is captured straight away. The settings button shows while the mouse is free. **Opening the inventory, a loot screen or the heal picker frees the cursor**; closing it captures it again. After the raid ends the cursor stays free for the results screen.
  - **Which controls show** follows the last pointer used: a fine pointer with hover (a mouse or trackpad) starts as desktop; a touch switches to the touch controls and a mouse click switches back, so a touchscreen laptop or an iPad with a trackpad works either way.
  - **A separate mouse sensitivity** (settings, default 1), since settings will sync across devices and a trackpad needs a different speed from a thumb. Settings shows only the one for how you're playing, and hides "Edit button layout" on desktop.
  - In the inventory: click, double-click and drag, with R to turn an item while dragging.
- **Controller (PC) (the user's request, 2026-11-08; raids built in CP-1, menus are CP-2):** a PS4 controller on a PC browser plays the whole game, raids, menus and inventory, with no mouse. **PC browsers only for now** (Chrome first), not the phone or Safari. It uses a common shooter layout, with **lean on the D-pad** (left and right; toggle lean works best). Menus use a virtual cursor that snaps to tiles and buttons. **Text fields stay keyboard-only** (sign-in, search, adding a friend), so there's no on-screen keyboard (both the user's calls, 2026-11-08). If an action doesn't fit the buttons, or something can't be reached with a pad, stop and raise it with the user rather than working around it. The button budget, proposed layout and sessions are in roadmap.md → PC controller. **In a raid (CP-1):** △ tapped takes out your other primary (or whichever one you carry, from the sidearm or knife). L3 sprints until you stop pushing forwards, aim or fire. Holding L1 or R1 opens the picker; the D-pad or the right stick moves through it, and letting go uses the highlighted one. Aim assist slows more on a pad than on touch, because a stick can't flick back onto a target as a thumb can. **On a pad it also follows (the user's OK, 2026-11-08: "standard controller stuff"):** while the crosshair is on an enemy and you're pushing either stick, the view turns with the enemy by a share of how fast it crosses your view (whether it moves or you strafe). The share is 30/45/60% for low/medium/high, half that from the hip, and Off turns it off. A still pad never aims for you. It's capped at 1.5 rad/s, so a close target never yanks the view. It works only on a pad, never on touch or mouse. The values are a first guess, to tune with the real pad. A pad press doesn't unlock sound in Chrome, so a pad player needs one click or key press per page load, and the raid says so if sound is still off. **Menus (CP-2):** a cursor (an amber dot, with a ring round what it's on) appears whenever a menu or screen is up, and hides while you play. The left stick moves it, slow on a small push and quick when held, and it snaps to the nearest button, tile or cell when you let go; the D-pad steps to the next one (or one cell in a grid). ✕ clicks, and held while moving it drags; □ moves an item where a double-tap would; △ opens its details, or turns an item you're dragging; ○ goes back (drops a drag where it was, closes details, settings, the bag, or the page); L1/R1 change tab (stash pages, vendors, the raid screen's tabs); the right stick scrolls; Options closes the screen (on the main menu it opens settings); the touchpad closes the bag. Sliders: ✕ grabs one, the D-pad or stick sets it, ✕ or ○ lets go. The press that brings the cursor up only shows it. **Polish (CP-3):** the pad rumbles when you fire (harder for a harder-kicking gun), when you're hit (harder for a bigger hit) and for explosions near you (full inside the blast, fading to nothing at four times its radius), with a Rumble setting, on by default. Settings has a Controller section with the button layout, worded for your aim, crouch and lean settings. In gamepad mode the hand slots, hints and inventory help name pad buttons, not keys.
- **Landscape only:** a "rotate your phone (and turn off rotation lock)" overlay shows whenever the phone is in portrait, and the game pauses. It covers **every screen**, the menu, stash and raid screens included, from the moment the page loads (fixed 2026-11-06: the menu used to draw over it, so a phone opened upright showed the menu sideways), with a phone icon turning on its side. Only the sign-in screen shows over it, where typing is easier upright.
- **Safari workarounds:** unlock audio on the first tap, run in standalone mode from the home screen (no browser UI), and disable pinch and double-tap zoom with CSS and the viewport settings. **Zoom (2026-10-26):** iOS ignores `user-scalable=no` and a double tap still zoomed the game on the phone, so `app/no-zoom.ts` also cancels Safari's pinch gesture events and a second `touchend` within 350 ms. It blocks nothing while the page is zoomed in, so you can always pinch back out.
