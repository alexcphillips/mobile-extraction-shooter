# Tech stack: Zombies mode

Part of the [tech-stack.md index](../tech-stack.md). Record decisions here, in the topic they belong to.

## Zombies mode
2026-11-04, `sim/zombies.ts` and `content/zombies.ts`. See design.md → Zombies.
- **`content/zombies/<id>.json`** (format 1): `name`, `map`, `kit` (a starting kit), `zombie` (`name`, `outfit`, `claws`: a melee item, `zombie-claws`), `rounds` (`first`, `perRound`, `maxAlive`, `spawnEvery`, `breakTime`, `health` and `healthPerRound` as multiples of a player's HP; the run settings were taken out on 2026-11-04, still format 1 since nothing else used it), `points` (`start`, `hit`, `kill`, `meleeKill`), `player` (`health`, `regenDelay`, `regenRate`), `barricades` (`rebuildTime`, `rebuildPoints`, `rebuildCap`, `tearTime`), `powerups` (`chance`, `perRound`, `lifetime`, `duration`, `carpenterPoints`, `minigun`, `weights` by `POWERUPS` kind), `spareMags` and `box` (`{ weight, item }` guns). The client's `loadZombies` checks the map, its zombie spawns, the kit, the claws, the box's guns and that the minigun has `infiniteAmmo`.
- **Map nodes** (map format still 3: new optional nodes and fields): markers `zombieSpawn` (`radius`, `window`: a barricade's id beside it, resolved to its path like a patrol), `wallBuy` (`item`, `cost`, `ammoCost`; yaw turns +z out of the wall, and `render/wall-buys.ts` hangs the gun's model on the wall face it finds with a ray behind the marker), `mysteryBox` (`cost`; the box itself is ordinary geometry; `ammoRefill` was taken out on 2026-11-04). **`barricade`** (`id`, `pos` the middle of the sill in the middle of the wall, yaw +z inside, `size` [width, height above the sill], `planks`, default 6) compiles to `CompiledMap.barricades` and a **blocker** in `WorldDef.blockers`: a solid filling the opening that movement and the nav grid respect but every ray passes (`CollisionWorld.firstBlocker`; blockers come last, after glass), so players never get out, and shots, claws and sight go through. The wall's opening is authored separately to match. A door may have a **`cost`** instead of a `key` (not both): it starts locked, and `useDoor` buys it with points (`bought`, or `poor`), setting `DoorState.gone`, which `syncDoors` passes to `CollisionWorld.setDoorGone` (its solid moved away, as broken glass's is) and the renderer hides; a gone door can't be used. The nav grid is built with cost doors gone (never swung open), so a couch's swing never blocks the stairs. A door's **`look`** (a prefab id, compiled into `CompiledDoor.look` shapes in its frame) draws it as that instead of a slab, never swinging: the couches. Its `size` and `thickness` are still its solid, so they're made tall enough not to vault. **`name`** is what the use button calls it ("Clear the couch"). All are in the map editor's marker tool, which draws barricades boarded up full. **A barricade is placed by clicking a wall** (2026-11-05, after one placed on a wall face came out buried in it, invisible): it goes in the middle of the wall's thickness at the sill (a window's own sill and size if clicked in a wall node's opening, else 0.9 m and 1.4 × 1.1 m), facing the side clicked; anywhere else it goes where clicked as before. The editor also draws its opening's outline and an arrow pointing inside through walls, since the wall hides the planks until it has a window to match. **Outside a zombies game** (a play-test of a map with barricades but no zombie spawns), barricades are still drawn, boarded up full (`barricadeWindows`), as their blockers are still there. **The marker tool's mystery box comes with its box** (2026-11-05, the user's request): it places a group named "Mystery box" holding the `mystery-box` prefab (Two Rooms' box: a dark base, a purple box and a gold lid, slots `wood-dark`, `paint`, `glow`) where clicked, and the marker 1 m in front of it, facing it, as the game expects; the box can still be any geometry. **The editor's zombies checkbox** (2026-11-05, the user's request), beside the marker picker, swaps which markers it offers: an extraction map's (enemy spawns, patrols, loot containers, loose loot, extractions, quest markers) for a zombies map's (zombie spawns, barricades, wall guns, the mystery box); player spawns and doors are in both. A door shows its key in an extraction map and its points cost in a zombies one (either still shows if set). It's an editor setting, not saved in the map: it starts ticked when the map already has any zombies-only marker. **Play ▸ on a map with zombie spawns plays a zombies game** (2026-11-05), with the rules and kit of the zombies mode that uses the map (else the first mode's); the editor refuses to play-test a map with no player spawn unless it starts at the camera target. A zombies map has no extraction (the content test expects one only on maps without zombie spawns).
- **State:** `SimState.zombies` (`ZombiesState`, null or missing in a raid) holds a copy of the rules, the spawns, buy spots and windows (`WindowState`: place, size, `max` and `planks`, and the rebuild and tear progress), the round, zombies left to appear, the timer, and each player's points, kills, plank points this round (`rebuilt`) and the tick they were last hurt. `startZombies` sets it up in place of `startRaid`. `step` runs `buy` (`PlayerInput.buy`, an index into `buys`; `buyInReach`, `spotPrice`) and `rebuild` (`PlayerInput.rebuild`, held; `windowInReach`, `windowSpot`) with the other presses, and `updateZombies` after bleeding, before enemies think: scoring this tick's damage and death events, players' health bars and refill, clearing zombies 4 s dead out of `entities`, and the rounds. Events: `round`, `buy` and `plank`.
- **One health bar:** `Health.pool`, its most HP, set on every zombies player: `dealDamage` puts all damage on the chest's HP with no spillover, `shootCharacter` skips the zone multiplier, and `woundZone` never starts bleeding. The client's `Vitals` shows a plain bar instead of the body.
- **Zombie AI:** an ordinary enemy (`spawnEnemy`) with `AiState.zombie` (`ZombieMind`: `run`, seconds `dead`, and `window`, its window until it's in), its claws in the knife slot and in hand, and its HP scaled by the round. `updateAi` gives it `hunt` instead of perceiving and `behave`. With a window, `breakIn`: `moveTo` the window's outside spot, then claw any player in reach on the other side, else tear a plank off every `tearTime`, and with none left start a scripted vault (`Character.vaulting`, which collides with nothing) to the inside spot. Inside: the nearest reachable player (picked again each second, via nav components), `moveTo` along the nav grid, swing when in reach and facing; no path is planned mid-vault. Doors open as for any enemy; a locked one cuts the nav grid, which with the blockers is also what decides which spawns are live.
- **Zombies admin tools** (dev only): `AdminCheats.infinitePoints` (optional) makes `buy` and `useDoor` free; the client keeps it and the start-with guns in `mes.zombiesCheats` (`readZombieCheats`, `applyZombieCheats` in `admin-cheats.ts`; the admin page's Zombies tab). `startPlay` gives those guns with `adminGiveGun` on a new game. The in-game panel (`play/zombies-admin.ts`, DOM, opened by its button or K, which holds you still like a dev screen) queues actions that `startPlay` does straight after the next tick with `adminPowerup`, `adminGiveGun`, points and `killCharacter`, so their events show; like the debug hurt and heal, that's solo only. The couches' look is `content/prefabs/couch-fallen.json`, the upright couch's boxes turned 80° back about its back edge and 9° askew (worked out by a script, since a prefab placement can't tilt).
- **Power-ups:** `ZombiesState.drops` (`PowerupDrop`: kind, place, seconds left), `nextDrop`, `dropped` (this round), `instaKill` and `doublePoints` (seconds left) and `machines` (`DeathMachine` by player: seconds left, the first primary slot's gun it replaced and the hand). `score` rolls a drop on each kill (`dropPowerup`); `powerups` ticks them, picks them up (`ZOMBIES.pickupReach`) and ends them; `applyPowerup` does each. Insta-kill is in `woundZone` (any hit on a zombie becomes a killing one), double points in `pointsScale`, and the death machine is the `death-machine` item, a weapon with **`infiniteAmmo`** (`freeAmmo` in `sim/weapons.ts`). `buy` refuses a new gun (`busy`) while one is held. Event: `powerup` (`dropped`, `picked`, `gone`, `ended`). The client draws drops with `render/powerups.ts` (a small pool of meshes, one look per kind) and lists the running ones on the HUD's round line.
- **The Ion Blaster:** a pistol-held grenade launcher (`launchSpeed` 70, `armDistance` 0: an impact fuse) firing `ion-bolt`, a grenade with its own **`caliber`** (`ion`; new optional field, so only launchers of that caliber fire it, and `throwable` keeps the grenade button from throwing it) and a **`stack`** (only such rounds may stack; `stackSize` counts it). In zombies mode `woundZone` keeps a player's explosions off players.
- **Blast guns:** `WeaponDef.blast` (`range`, `angle`, `push`; an optional field, so item format still 10): `blastShot` in `sim/weapons.ts` gives every living enemy (never a player) in range and cone, with a clear ray from the eye, a killing chest hit and a fling, and pushes a `shot` event with `blast` set to its range; the client draws a gust (`Effects.gust`) instead of a tracer. `gale-cannon` fires `gale-charge` (caliber `gale`).
- **Client:** `startPlay` takes `zombies` (a `ZombiesDef`) with a profile made from its kit; it skips loot, quests, the timer, saving and resuming, hides the bag button and never opens the inventory, adds and removes zombie bodies as they come and go, draws the planks (`render/barricades.ts`, one instanced mesh, `frustumCulled` off since its bounds would be worked out with every plank hidden), shows "Round n · points" where the timer goes, and the use button buys (`buy`) or, held, rebuilds (`rebuild`; `Controls.interactHeld`).
